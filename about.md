### Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of optimization and machine learning:

- **Multi-Objective Optimization (MOO)** — algorithms and theory for problems with multiple competing objectives
- **Multi-Task Learning (MTL)** — learning shared representations across related tasks
- **Neural Architecture Search (NAS)** — automated design of neural network architectures
